# DoorSense Demo Script — Under 3 Minutes

## 0:00–0:15 — Problem

“Doorbell and motion alerts are often audio-first and ambiguous. DoorSense gives people who cannot rely on sound a calm, visual explanation of what happened and what to do next.”

Show the accessible home screen.

## 0:15–0:35 — Prove Ring runtime

Show the supported Ring simulator/API/device event being triggered, then show the event arriving in DoorSense. Keep the source visible long enough for the judge to recognize the integration.

## 0:35–1:05 — Possible delivery

Trigger the delivery scenario. Show the large card: “Possible delivery.” Show timestamp, evidence, and a low-risk next action. Do not say “delivery confirmed” unless the source actually provides that fact.

## 1:05–1:35 — Familiar context

Trigger a configured, consent-based familiar-context scenario. Use cautious wording such as “Possible familiar-context match.” Show the evidence used and the option to review the door without implying identity certainty.

## 1:35–2:00 — Ambiguity handled safely

Trigger an event with insufficient evidence. Show “Unclassified visitor” or “Motion detected, person not confirmed.” Explain that DoorSense does not guess or label a person as dangerous.

## 2:00–2:20 — Accessibility and deduplication

Show visual-only mode, keyboard focus, high contrast, and repeated events grouped into one timeline item.

## 2:20–2:45 — Engineering proof

Show the repository briefly: Ring adapter, typed schema, deterministic classifier, tests, and local free stack. Show one test command and its passing result.

## 2:45–2:59 — Close

“DoorSense makes Ring events more understandable, accessible, and respectful of uncertainty. It turns a raw alert into useful guidance without making unsafe claims.”

## Recording rules

- Use English narration or captions.
- Keep the best material in the first 30 seconds.
- Avoid copyrighted music or third-party footage.
- Do not show secrets, private addresses, or unnecessary personal data.
- Verify the final video URL is public and remains under three minutes.
